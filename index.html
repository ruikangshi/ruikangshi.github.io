About Me

Hi, I'm Ruikang (Bobby) Shi — a systems engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Grainger College, class of 2029. I'm drawn to the engineering behind automobiles: the structures that keep cars light and stiff, the systems that make them fast, and the details that make them feel alive.

I've had the opportunity to work across several domains — designing solar vehicle structures with Illini Solar Car, building a business from zero, and developing the kind of hands-on, systems-level thinking that automotive engineering demands.

Outside of engineering, I'm drawn to leadership, mentorship, and making things with my hands — from woodworking to circuits. I believe the best automotive engineers aren't just analysts; they're builders who understand why every gram and every millimeter matters.
